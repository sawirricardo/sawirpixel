This policy explains what {operator} ("we", "us") collects when you use {app}, why, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. It applies alongside our [terms of service](/terms).

## What we collect

- **Your account:** your name, email address, and password (stored only as a one-way hash, never in a form we can read), and when you verified your email.
- **Organizations:** the organizations you create or join, your roles and permissions in them, invitations you send or receive, and the limits set for them.
- **Generations:** your prompts, the size and quality you chose, the reference image links you add, the status of each image, its estimated and actual cost, and the link to the finished image while it's valid.
- **Credits:** every purchase, amount set aside and charge on your organization's balance, and who made it. We don't receive or store card details.
- **Personal access tokens:** each token's note, organization, scopes, expiry and when it was last used. The token itself is stored only as a one-way hash.
- **Notifications** we show you in the app, such as when an image is ready.
- **Technical data:** your IP address and browser details in server logs, used to keep the service secure and working, and to limit how often requests can be made.

## Why we use it

- To provide {app}: generating your images, showing your history, and running your organization.
- To bill accurately: estimating prices, setting aside credits and charging what each image actually cost.
- To send the emails the service needs, such as verifying your address, resetting your password and invitations.
- To keep the service safe, including preventing abuse and enforcing our terms.
- To meet legal obligations, such as keeping financial records.

We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or use your prompts or images to train models.

## Who we share it with

- **Our image generation provider** receives your prompt, chosen options and reference image links to make each image, and hosts the finished image for 24 hours.
- **Our email provider** receives your address and the content of emails we send you.
- **Our hosting providers** store and process data on our behalf to run the service.
- **Your organization:** its owners and admins can see members' names, email addresses and generations, and the organization's credit history.
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it.

## Cookies

We only use cookies needed for {app} to work: one keeps you signed in, and one protects forms against cross-site request forgery. There are no analytics or advertising cookies.

## How long we keep it

- Account and organization data: for as long as the account or organization exists.
- Finished images: we don't keep them. The provider's link expires 24 hours after an image is generated.
- Generation and credit records: kept for as long as needed for billing, tax and accounting, including after an organization is deleted.
- Server logs: kept for a limited period for security and troubleshooting.

## Your choices and rights

You can update your name and email address in your profile at any time. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or to object to how we use it. To make a request, contact us using the details below; we'll respond within the time the law requires. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

## Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, passwords and tokens are hashed, and access to data is limited to what's needed to run the service. No system is perfectly secure, but we'll tell you without undue delay if a breach affects your data.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll let account holders know by email or in the app.
